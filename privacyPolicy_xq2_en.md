# Xiang Yi Qi Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

## Overview

Xiang Yi Qi does not collect, upload, or sell any of your personal information.

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS, iPadOS, and macOS versions of Xiang Yi Qi (the "App"), provided by gangtaoyu ("we", "us"). By using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and agree to this policy.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. Specifically:

- The App requires no registration or sign-in, and does not ask for your name, phone number, email address, or any other identifying information.
- The App does not access your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising identifier.
- All games are computed entirely on your device. Your games and usage records are never sent to our servers.
- We do not track you, and we do not share, sell, or transfer your data to any third party.

## Data Stored on Your Device

To make the App work, the following data is stored only on your device, where we cannot access it:

- Your level and rating points
- Unfinished games and game progress
- In-app settings

Deleting the App removes all of this data. If you have device backups turned on (for example, iCloud Backup), this data may be stored by Apple as part of your device backup, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

## Third-Party Services and Permissions

The App contains no advertising and does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs. The App does not request any sensitive system permissions.

When you download the App from the App Store, Apple may process download and crash diagnostic information under its own privacy policy. That information is collected and managed by Apple. Whether diagnostic data is shared with developers is controlled by you in your system settings, and it contains nothing that identifies you.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Minors should use the App under the guidance of a parent or guardian.

## Changes to This Policy

If changes to the App affect how data is handled, we will update this policy and publish the new effective date on this page. Significant changes will be announced in the App or in the App Store release notes.
